## Ch1 — The Shrine

**Level:** 1 · **Location:** `loc-shrine`  
**Load path:** `Ch1_Prologue/The_Shrine`

### Setup

PC and younger sister **Sera** (`npc-sera`) are traveling (default: toward Hollowbridge). Sera notices strange light or whispered sounds from an abandoned roadside shrine / burial mound and insists on looking. PC may argue; she goes anyway; PC follows.

### Beats

1. **Approach** — Cold air, wrong silence, ritual chalk or black candles.
2. **Discovery** — **Malachara** (`npc-malachar`) mid-ritual at the center. Gaunt, hollow-cheeked, cold voice.
3. **Curse** — She targets the **PC**. Sera steps in front; curse lands on Sera.  
   - If PC attacks or blocks: allow **one** action/reaction; curse still finds Sera.  
   - Do **not** let them prevent the curse or capture Malachara.
4. **Escape** — Malachara finishes and flees (shadow, teleport, or vanishing into the mound). No prolonged fight.
5. **Aftermath** — Sera pale, skin beginning to fade/decay; guilt; urgency for help.

### Story elements

- **ev1-discovery** — Ritual found. *NPC:* npc-malachar.
- **ev1-curse** — Sera takes the curse meant for the PC.
- **ev1-escape** — Malachara gone; revenge is a **future** campaign hook only.
- **tr1-travel** — Load `Ch1_Prologue/Travel_to_Hollowbridge`.

### Time

**No deductions** until the curse is applied. When applied: campaign counter **starts at 35**.

### XP

**25 XP** for completing the prologue scene (award outside combat).
