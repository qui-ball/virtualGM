## Ch1 — Travel to Hollowbridge

**Level:** 1 · **Locations:** road → `loc-hollowbridge`  
**Load path:** `Ch1_Prologue/Travel_to_Hollowbridge`

### Setup

After the shrine: PC must get Sera to help. **Default:** they were already headed to Hollowbridge; continue there and seek the temple (`loc-temple`, Matron Evaine).

### Beats

1. Short travel montage — Sera weakens; townsfolk may stare if they meet anyone.
2. Arrive Hollowbridge at dusk or under gray sky; dark, urgent tone.
3. Direct PC to the **temple** first (Evaine). If they go straight to Aldric, he can note the temple would have sent them — allow skip if it fits pacing.

### Time

Apply travel deduction **−2 or −3** after the curse counter has started (35 → 33 or 32).

### Transition

- Load `Ch2_Dragon_Scale/Hollowbridge_Evaine_Aldric` when they seek help in town.
- Ingredient chapters (Ch2–Ch4) may be pursued in **any order** after Aldric gives the list — but Evaine → Aldric should happen once early so the quest is clear.

### Story elements

- **tr1-to-ch2** — Reach Hollowbridge; seek Evaine / Aldric.
