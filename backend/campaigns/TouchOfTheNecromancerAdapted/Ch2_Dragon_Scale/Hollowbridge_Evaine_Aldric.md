## Ch2 — Hollowbridge: Evaine & Aldric

**Level:** 1–2 · **Locations:** `loc-temple`, `loc-aldric-tower`  
**Load path:** `Ch2_Dragon_Scale/Hollowbridge_Evaine_Aldric`

### Matron Evaine (`npc-evaine`) — temple

- Stern, regretful; grey-streaked hair; firm voice.
- Recognizes **necromantic** curse: divine magic can ease suffering, not break death-woven bindings.
- Insists PC go to **Aldric the Grey** quickly.
- Offer to keep **Sera** at the temple while the PC gathers ingredients.

### Aldric the Grey (`npc-aldric`) — tower

- Reclusive, prickly; cluttered tower.
- Gives three ingredients (**any order**):
  1. Dragon scale (or acceptable substitute)
  2. Mercury (pure or impure ore → vial)
  3. Mandrake roots
- Stresses importance **once**. Does **not** mention impure backfire risk.
- Partial returns (1–2 ingredients): **refuses** ritual; clock keeps running.
- Sera may stay here or at the temple during Ch2–Ch4.

### Optional Sera deterioration

- Counter **≤20:** noticeably worse.
- Counter **≤10:** barely conscious.
Use for tension; no rule change.

### Story elements

- **ev2-evaine** — Temple cannot break the curse; send to Aldric.
- **ev2-aldric-list** — Full ingredient list; no backfire warning.
- **tr2-ingredients** — Player chooses first leg:  
  - Scale → `Ch2_Dragon_Scale/Torval_and_Northreach`  
  - Mercury → `Ch3_Mercury/Vesper_and_Mine`  
  - Mandrake → `Ch4_Mandrake/Mara` or `Ch4_Mandrake/Elven_Forest`

### Time

No required deduction for these conversations unless the PC delays (optional −1).
