## Ch4 — Elven Forest (Mandrake)

**Level:** 1–3 · **Location:** `loc-forest`  
**Load path:** `Ch4_Mandrake/Elven_Forest`

### Setup

PC forages for mandrake in the elven wood. Travel Hollowbridge → forest (**−2**).

### Forage

- Call for **Wit** or **Finesse** to identify the right plant.
- Success → **pure** mandrake.
- Failure / wilted roots → **impure** (optional impure variant; use for symmetry with scale/mercury).

### Elven rangers (`enemy-elven-ranger`)

- 1–2 rangers may challenge trespassers.
- Prefer warning / drive-off over slaughter.
- **Ranger or elf PC:** advantage on Presence (parley) or Finesse (avoid), or rangers allow passage with a warning.
- Combat is non-boss; 0 HP → recover + setback (empty-handed / escorted out).

### Story elements

- **ev4-forest** — Forage; possible ranger confrontation.
- **ev4-mandrake-impure** — Wrong plant / wilted roots.
- **tr4-next** — Roots obtained → next ingredient or Ch5. Return travel **−2**.

### Time

- Travel each way: **−2**.
- Short/long rests as global rules.

### XP

- Mandrake obtained (if not already awarded via Mara): **50 XP**
- Rangers encountered: **25 XP**
