## Ch4 — Mara (Mandrake)

**Level:** 1–3 · **Location:** `loc-herbalist`  
**Load path:** `Ch4_Mandrake/Mara`

### Mara (`npc-mara`)

- Weather-worn face, green-stained fingers; calm, observant; hard but fair bargains.
- Has or can get **mandrake roots**.
- Price negotiable (gold, favor, small errand, Presence checks).

### Outcome

- Successful deal → mandrake **pure**.
- If PC cannot afford / fails: steer to forest (`Ch4_Mandrake/Elven_Forest`) or allow future payment to avoid deadlock.

### Story elements

- **ev4-mara** — Negotiate for mandrake.
- **tr4-forest** — Optional: PC chooses forage instead → load `Ch4_Mandrake/Elven_Forest`.
- **tr4-next** — Roots obtained → next ingredient or Ch5.

### Time

Usually no travel cost (in Hollowbridge). Short/long rests if they delay.

### XP

Mandrake obtained via Mara: **50 XP** (award once when roots secured, not twice if also forest).
