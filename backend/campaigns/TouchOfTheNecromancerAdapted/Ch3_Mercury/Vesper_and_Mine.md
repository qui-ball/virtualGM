## Ch3 — Vesper & the Mercury Mine

**Level:** 1–2 · **Locations:** `loc-alchemist`, `loc-mine`  
**Load path:** `Ch3_Mercury/Vesper_and_Mine`

### Vesper (`npc-vesper`) — shop

- Slight, quick hands, chemical smell; short practical speech.
- Knows an **abandoned mine** with mercury ore.
- Gives a **detector** (points toward mercury-bearing rock).
- Will prepare a vial from ore the PC brings; keeps surplus; **no charge**.

### Mine beats (`loc-mine`)

1. Travel to mine (**−2**).
2. Enter shafts — cold, wet, wrong; detector ticks near veins.
3. **Undead** (non-boss): 1–2 `enemy-zombie` and/or `enemy-skeleton`. Load `Appendix_Enemies/Enemy_Stat_Blocks`.
4. Collect ore:
   - Careful / pure vein → **pure** mercury.
   - Rushed, contaminated, or deliberate impure vein → **impure**.
5. Return to Vesper (**−2**); he prepares the vial.

### Non-boss 0 HP

Full HP/mana recover + setback (driven out, ore lost, detector damaged).

### Story elements

- **ev3-vesper** — Detector and directions.
- **ev3-mine-undead** — Undead in the mine.
- **ev3-ore-choice** — Pure vs impure ore.
- **tr3-next** — Vial obtained → next ingredient or Ch5.

### Time

- Travel each way: **−2**.
- Optional mine delay: **−1**.
- Short/long rests as global rules.

### XP

- Mercury obtained: **50 XP**
- Undead encounter: **50 XP**
